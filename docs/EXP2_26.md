# EXP2.26: Rho-Adaptive Outer Optimizer, Development Screen

## Question

Can a probe-free controller that measures round-to-round merged-delta
autocorrelation online match the best fixed outer-momentum setting at every
sync horizon without knowing the horizon?

## Method

`rho-adaptive` (syncer commits `4f0b1eb`, `1eca19c`, plus the preview
bit-exactness fix): the optimizer buffer stores the previously applied
direction; each commit measures ρ = cos(delta_t, buf) and applies the
memoryless step η/(1−μ_eff·ρ) with μ_eff = clamp(2(1−ρ), 0, μ_max=0.9),
amplification capped at 4×. Previews are bit-identical to applied steps
(two production integrity checks caught earlier drafts). The tape logs ρ
per commit in the history-ratio column.

Three arms on the EXP2.25 protocol (seed 223, sync, LR 0.175, equal total
work): H ∈ {16, 64, 256}. Verda spot 1×RTX PRO 6000, ~$2.

## Result

| H | μ=0 | μ=0.5 | μ=0.9 | rho-adaptive | Online mean ρ (RDA) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | **1.3519** | 1.3632 | 1.4383 | 1.3597 | 0.447 |
| 64 | 1.3578 | 1.3614 | 1.4193 | **1.3582** | 0.289 |
| 256 | 1.3805 | **1.3674** | 1.3977 | 1.3723 | 0.221 |

- Never worse than second-best at any horizon; never catastrophic. Ties
  the best arm at H=64 (+0.0004), gives back 0.008 at H=16 and 0.005 at
  H=256.
- Worst-case regret across horizons: rho-adaptive 0.008 vs 0.013 (μ=0),
  0.011 (μ=0.5), 0.086 (μ=0.9) — the controller roughly halves the best
  fixed policy's worst-case regret with zero per-horizon tuning.
- Mean across horizons: 1.3634, tied with μ=0 (1.3634), ahead of μ=0.5
  (1.3640).

## The ρ-Convention Finding

Online ρ measured on production RDA-merged deltas is far lower than the
plain-candidate-average proxy used in EXP2.25 (H=16: ≈0.45–0.6 online vs
0.98 offline) and *decreases* with H in both conventions. Consequences:

1. The EXP2.25 retrodiction table used the plain-average convention; the
   law's inputs must be restated with the RDA convention that the momentum
   buffer actually integrates (adjudication delegated to an independent
   review; may require a second mechanism term for the μ=0.9 blowup).
2. The controller's κ=2 rule was calibrated on the wrong convention, so it
   ran ~2× amplification at H=16 where ~1× was optimal — explaining its
   0.008 gap there. Recalibration against RDA-ρ is the obvious v2.

## Limitations

Single seed, development screen only (the controller was designed after
seeing the fixed grid on the same seed); fresh-seed confirmation across
all three horizons is required before any claim. Same model/data/LoRA
scope as the series.
