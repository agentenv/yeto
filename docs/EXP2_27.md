# EXP2.27: Fresh-Seed Confirmation and LR-Confound Test

## Question

Do the EXP2.25 momentum-crossover corners replicate on a seed the series
has never touched, and does a tuned-η memoryless baseline dissolve the
fixed-η momentum advantage at H=256 — as the corrected two-term law
(EXP2_25.md, Correction section) predicts?

## Setup

```text
provider: Verda spot, 4x A100
protocol: sync zero-delay, identical to EXP2.25 (Qwen3.5-9B LoRA r2,
  Capybara, m4 strict quorum, equal total work)
seed: 251/251251 (fresh; preregistered before any arm ran)
```

Six arms: H ∈ {16, 256} × {μ=0 η=0.175, μ=0.5 η=0.175, μ=0 η=0.28}. The
η=0.28 arms are the memoryless tuned-LR baseline (SGD-0.28) that the
aligned term of the two-term law says μ=0.5 should approximate at long
horizons. One spot preemption hit mid-run; the S3-resume wrapper completed
the remaining arms. Whole run ≈ $8.

**Design change:** the plan originally included controller-v1 arms.
They were withdrawn before launch on advisor guidance — the controller's
κ=2 rule is known miscalibrated (EXP2.26), and burning the fresh seed on
it would have left no untouched seed for a frozen v2. Seed 251 therefore
carries only the preregistered fixed-policy arms.

## Result

64-row eval loss:

| H | μ=0 (η=0.175) | μ=0.5 (η=0.175) | SGD-0.28 (μ=0) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | **1.627433** | 1.641685 | 1.636382 |
| 256 | 1.645687 | 1.640791 | **1.639274** |

Preregistered findings:

1. **The crossover replicates on a second untouched seed, in both
   directions.** At fixed η=0.175, μ=0 wins H=16 by `0.0143` and μ=0.5
   wins H=256 by `0.0049` — the same signs as seed 223 in EXP2.25.
2. **The LR-confound test lands where the two-term law points.** Tuned-η
   memoryless SGD-0.28 slightly beats μ=0.5 at H=256 (1.6393 vs 1.6408)
   and loses at H=16 to η=0.175 μ=0. Momentum's long-horizon benefit at
   fixed η is therefore primarily an effective-LR effect via the aligned
   term of the corrected law (see EXP2_25.md, Correction section), not a
   memory effect per se.
3. **No fixed η wins at both horizons** (0.175 takes H=16, 0.28 takes
   H=256), which is exactly the gap the rho-adaptive controller exists to
   close.

## Limitations

Two seeds total (223, 251); 64-row eval and no confidence intervals yet;
same model/data/LoRA-r2 scope as the whole series.
